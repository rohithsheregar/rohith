# Daily Dev Roadmap: Full-Stack, Backend and DSA

One file that answers "what do I do today?" Pick a mode, pick from its menu, do it, log it, close the laptop.

- Start date: `____-__-__`
- Current stage: `0`
- Next unchecked topic: `____________`

---

## 1. Start here every day (60 seconds)

1. Pick today's mode from the table below. Choose by the lower of your energy and your free time.
2. Open that mode's menu (section 3) and pick items. Write them in today's log.
3. Set a timer. Phone out of reach. Only open links from this README.
4. Do the items. Check the box in the roadmap when a topic passes its check.
5. Fill the log (section 8), add new topics to the revision queue (section 5), stop.

You may upgrade a day mid-way (Low to Medium to High). You may never downgrade with guilt: any mode counts as a done day.

---

## 2. Modes

One **topic** = one checkbox in the roadmap = one focused session of about 35 minutes (learn plus practise).

| Mode | Time | New topics | Shape | DSA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Low | 45 min | 0 | Revise and self-test only | Re-solve 1-2 failed problems |
| Medium | 60-90 min | 1-2 | Learn one topic, apply it, log it | 0-1 new problem |
| High | 3-5 h | 4-6 | Learn, build, stretch, assess | 3-5 problems |

How to pick:

- Slept badly, busy, or flat: Low.
- Normal day with an hour or so: Medium.
- Rested, motivated, a free evening or weekend: High.
- Unsure: start Medium; after 20 minutes decide whether to stop, stay or extend.

The floor is never zero. If a day collapses, one quiz or one DSA problem keeps the streak.

## 3. Menus

### Low day menu (45 min, pick 3 or 4, no new videos or docs)

| Pick | Item | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ ] | Revision queue: do everything due today (section 5) | 10 min |
| [ ] | Self-quiz: 5 questions from the current stage's "Check yourself" | 10 min |
| [ ] | Re-solve 1-2 DSA problems you failed before, from a blank page | 15 min |
| [ ] | Explain one concept in five sentences without notes | 5 min |
| [ ] | Tidy: clean notes, improve a commit message or the project README | 5 min |
| [ ] | Choose tomorrow's items and write them in the log | 3 min |

### Medium day menu (60-90 min)

| Block | What | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Plan: choose topic A (and optionally B), write the goal in the log | 5 min |
| 2 | Learn topic A from the stage's primary resource | 25-35 min |
| 3 | Apply: build or modify something in the stage project using topic A | 25-35 min |
| 4 | Log, add to revision queue, commit and push | 10 min |
| Optional | 1 new DSA problem, or topic B as a shorter repeat of blocks 2-3 | 20-30 min |

### High day menu (3-5 h, 4-6 topics)

Pick the blocks that fit your time. Full day is about 4.8 hours.

| Block | What | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | DSA: 3 new problems of one pattern, plus 1 revisit | 60 min |
| 2 | Topic A: learn and practise | 40 min |
| 3 | Topic B: learn and practise | 40 min |
| 4 | Build: ship one real feature in the stage project, with a test | 60-90 min |
| 5 | Stretch: one option from the best-of-the-best list below | 45 min |
| 6 | Assess and revise: revision queue plus a stage quiz | 30 min |

- 3 hours: blocks 1, 2, 4, 6.
- 4 hours: blocks 1, 2, 3, 4, 6.
- 5 hours: all six.

**Best-of-the-best stretch options, ranked by experience gained**

1. Ship and deploy: push the feature live, add it to the README with a screenshot.
2. Debug something hard: profile a slow endpoint or reproduce and fix a bug, then write what you learned.
3. System design exercise: design one system from the [System Design Primer](https://github.com/donnemartin/system-design-primer) on paper, then compare with its solution.
4. Mock interview: 45 minutes timed on one DSA medium plus explaining a project decision out loud.
5. Read a best-practice section from [Node.js Best Practices](https://github.com/goldbergyoni/nodebestpractices) and apply two items to your code.
6. Refactor with tests: improve one module and keep every test green.
7. Read real source: open the source of one library you use and write down how it works.
8. Write it up: a one-page note of a decision you made and why.

## 4. Rules that remove confusion

1. Open only the links listed in this README. Anything interesting goes in the **Parking lot** (section 10) and is reviewed on Sunday.
2. No new course, tool or framework until the current stage is gated.
3. A missed day is followed by a Low day. No catching up.
4. Finish and commit before you start something new.
5. Stage gate: every topic checked, "Check yourself" passed (4 of 5 or better), project deployed, README written. Then move on.
6. Weekly review every Sunday (template in section 9).
7. If you are stuck for 30 minutes, write the question in the log, move to the next item and ask or search later.

## 5. Revision system (spaced repetition)

When you finish a topic, add it to the queue. Revise it at these gaps:

| Revision | Due after learning |
| --- | --- |
| R1 | +1 day |
| R2 | +3 days |
| R3 | +7 days |
| R4 | +21 days |
| R5 | +60 days |

A revision is a quick test, not rereading: answer two questions or rebuild a tiny example from memory in 5 minutes. If you fail, reset the topic to R1.

### Assessment types

- **Quiz:** five questions, no notes, pass at 4 of 5.
- **Explain:** five sentences out loud as if teaching a friend.
- **Rebuild:** recreate a small example from a blank file.
- **Mock:** timed DSA or design question.
